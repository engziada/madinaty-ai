# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
- **Madinaty Residents:** Looking for smart local guides (Astro), real-time transport logs, and community news.
- **Parents:** Seeking trusted, certified AI and technology education for their children (ages 6–15) via the Innovation Hub.
- **Youth (16–18):** Seeking local summer internships and apprenticeships via SkillUp Shabab.
- **Local Business Owners & Home Chefs:** Looking for digital presence booster packages and community marketplace listings (Bas Douk, Sakn).

## Product Purpose
- Serve as the intelligence and connectivity layer for Madinaty's 700,000+ residents.
- Empower residents with smart tools, direct community commerce, and hyper-local guide services.
- Provide a trusted, verified framework for community learning and professional skill-sharing.

## Brand Personality
- **Modern & Visionary:** Guided by the Midnight Blue and Electric Teal Horizon theme, feeling advanced and futuristic.
- **Trustworthy & Warm:** Grounded in real-world community, keeping safety, verification, and family values at the center.
- **Organic & Community-Built:** Avoids standard commercial corporate language, feeling instead like a grassroots digital utility built by residents.

## Anti-references
- **SaaS Monoculture:** Cliché software landings with repeating identical icons/cards, sterile illustrations, and generic pricing tables.
- **Cold Corporate Portals:** Static layouts lacking community energy, interactive elements, or localized references.

## Design Principles
- **Hyper-Local Context:** Every feature and design element should celebrate Madinaty's districts, services, and community reality.
- **Visual Delight:** Leverage fluid animations, interactive maps, and micro-interactions to create a premium, engaging experience.
- **Supervised Trust:** Always make safety, expert supervision (e.g., Cairo University), and community badges visually clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Bilingual & RTL-First:** Seamless English and Arabic support, ensuring correct layout direction and reading flow.
- **High Readability:** Ensure copy and text elements maintain WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1) against dark backgrounds.
- **Motion Sensitivity:** Respect user preferences for reduced motion by offering static or crossfade fallbacks for core animations.
