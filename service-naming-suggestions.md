## Naming Principles Applied

1. **Egyptian Slang** - Uses everyday spoken Arabic for instant relatability
2. **Brevity** - 1-3 words maximum for easy recall
3. **Action-Oriented** - Names that imply doing/moving/benefit
4. **Emotional Hooks** - Nostalgia (اكل أمّك), empowerment (صوتك مسموع), belonging (ناس زيّك)
5. **Commercial Appeal** - Names that work in ads, social media, and word-of-mouth
