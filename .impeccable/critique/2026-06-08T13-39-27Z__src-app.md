A strong foundation with excellent layout and typography basics, but it's held back by small, tacky decorative details like excessive text gradients and bouncy animations.

- **Typography Updates**: The shift to Space Grotesk/Inter and Alexandria/Cairo makes the layout highly readable and modern.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Artificial Gradients**: The widespread use of `background-clip: text` gradients makes the platform feel like a generic AI template instead of a premium community portal.
  - *Fix*: Remove text gradients and rely on solid colors with varying font weights for emphasis.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable quieter`
- **[P2] Bouncy Easing & Layout Thrashing**: Animations using `cubic-bezier(0.175, ...)` and animating `width`/`height` feel dated and cause jank.
  - *Fix*: Replace with exponential easing (`ease-out-expo`) and use `transform` instead of layout properties.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable polish`
- **[P3] Em-dash Overuse**: Metadata and copy using 10+ em-dashes disrupt natural reading rhythms.
  - *Fix*: Rephrase or use standard punctuation (commas, colons).
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable clarify`
